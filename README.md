# OSTAR's Swords
> Steam link: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3676619843

## Description
Ostar's Swords is an expansive Terraria mod that adds a massive collection of unique, powerful, and wildly creative swords to your game. From classic fantasy blades and pop-culture references to absurdly fun concept weapons, this mod gives you a completely new arsenal for your playthroughs!

Features:
- Hundreds of new unique swords with special effects, custom projectiles, and abilities!
- New armor sets and classes, such as the colorful Damascus armor variations.
- Custom materials for crafting your perfect blade (Damascus Ore, Sword Matter, Orichalcon).
- Unique consumables and potions like Nord Mead and Skooma!
- Plenty of references to your favorite games, movies, and books!
- Unique swords that offer damage based on enemy's max health.

Whether you want to slice up enemies with the legendary Abyssal Rune Blade, rain chaos with explosive bullet swords, deal percentile damage with the Old Man's Blade, or just get a massive stat boost from questionable potions, Ostar's Swords has a weapon for every Terrarian!
- Have in mind that this mod is in early development, so expect bugs and missing features. Also, it's a bit OP, but that's intended.
